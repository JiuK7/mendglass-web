# Mendglass Brand System v1

### Brand handoff for `04 — Website & Portfolio Build`

This is the current **source of truth for the Mendglass visual identity**. It is mature enough to begin applying to the website. Exact page compositions, frontend effects and responsive implementation remain the responsibility of the Website & Portfolio chat.

---

## 1. Core brand direction

### Name

**Mendglass**

### Visual direction

**Optical Precision**

Mendglass combines:

-  warm editorial composition 
-  precise digital structure 
-  restrained optical/material effects 
-  modern technology cues 
-  hospitality-aware warmth 

The brand should feel:

**Precise · Clear · Crafted · Progressive · Assured**

It should be premium through execution rather than luxury signalling.

### Central visual principle

> **Clear at first glance. More considered when you look closer.**

Details should reward attention without obstructing comprehension.

### Core conceptual territory

**Mend**

-  refinement 
-  repair 
-  improvement 
-  resolution 

**Glass**

-  clarity 
-  lens 
-  surface 
-  transparency 
-  refraction 

The identity expresses these ideas abstractly rather than through literal broken-glass imagery.

# 2. Logo system

## Primary wordmark

Lowercase:

**`mendglass`**

The mark is based on clean contemporary grotesk typography with a custom **`d → g`** **transformation**.

The `d/g` junction uses a sharp diagonal negative-space transition that suggests:

-  refraction 
-  a glass edge 
-  one form resolving into another 
-  optical transformation 

The effect lives in the vector geometry itself.

### Wordmark rules

Use:

-  Carbon on light backgrounds 
-  Mineral on dark backgrounds 
-  generous clear space 
-  monochrome by default 

Do not add:

-  gradients 
-  shadows 
-  glass fills 
-  transparency inside the letters 
-  coloured shards 
-  extra symbols 
-  literal glass effects 

The surrounding interface can contain optical effects; the core wordmark remains simple.

# 8. Typography

## Primary family

**Instrument Sans**

Use for both:

-  headings 
-  body text 

No secondary font family is currently required.

This keeps the brand cohesive and prevents the identity from becoming overly styled.

---

## Relume typography setting

**Large / Medium / Spacious**

### Heading behaviour

-  Instrument Sans Medium 
-  large editorial scale 
-  relatively tight tracking 
-  confident rather than bold/heavy 
-  sentence case 

### Body behaviour

-  Instrument Sans Regular 
-  generous line height 
-  highly readable 
-  neutral and human 

### Metadata / system typography

Use small Instrument Sans Medium with:

-  optional uppercase 
-  increased tracking 
-  compact labels 

Examples:

`PROJECT / 01`

`LONDON / 2026`

`MOBILE / DESKTOP`

Use sparingly as a structural layer.

---

# 9. Typographic character

Mendglass typography operates in two modes.

### Editorial mode

Large, clear statements:

> Restaurant websites designed around
>
> bookings, menus and enquiries.

Human, spacious and confident.

### System mode

Small structural information:

> PROJECT / 01
>
> WEB / MOBILE
>
> LONDON / 2026

Precise and technical.

The tension between these two modes is part of the Optical Precision identity.

# 10. Buttons & forms

## Relume base

**Default**

### Radius

Restrained/modest radius.

Approximately the visual territory of:

**6–10px**

Avoid:

-  highly rounded bubble UI 
-  default pill buttons everywhere 

### Primary button

-  Carbon background 
-  Mineral text 
-  Instrument Sans Medium 

### Secondary button

-  transparent/light background 
-  thin border 
-  Carbon text 

### Forms

Prefer:

-  clear rectangular fields 
-  light neutral surfaces 
-  thin borders 
-  clear focus state 

### Shadows

Little or none.

The brand should use **edges, borders and surface contrast before elevation/shadows**.

# 11. Cards & images

## Relume base

**Outlined**

This best fits Optical Precision because structure comes from:

-  thin borders 
-  spacing 
-  geometry 
-  surface contrast 

rather than conventional filled cards.

### Radius

Use the same radius family as buttons/forms.

### Global principle

Not everything needs to become a card.

Open editorial layouts and uncontained imagery are encouraged where appropriate.

# 12. Surface language

Mendglass uses three conceptual surface levels:

### Base

Mineral, Carbon, Warm Stone, Mist or Deep Mineral backgrounds.

### Content

Typography, photography, standard interface elements.

### Optical layer

Selective translucent/refractive surfaces above content.

This third layer is the brand signature and should remain uncommon.

---

# 13. Glass / optical treatment

Glass is a **material**, not the theme of every component.

Use glass when something meaningful exists behind it.

Good contexts:

-  hero composition 
-  navigation over imagery 
-  selected project metadata 
-  image captions 
-  occasional information pane 
-  carefully chosen CTA/detail moment 

Possible behaviours:

-  restrained translucency 
-  slight background blur 
-  fine highlight edge 
-  subtle refraction 
-  light diffusion 
-  tiny optical displacement 

Avoid:

-  glass cards everywhere 
-  heavy frosted SaaS panels 
-  giant glow effects 
-  excessive blur 
-  fake 3D jelly surfaces 
-  using glass solely because the company is named Mendglass 

### Internal rule

> **If everything is glass, nothing is glass.**

---

# 14. Borders, shadows and radius

### Borders

Prefer:

-  fine hairline borders 
-  subtle contrast 
-  precise edge definition 

Edges should generally do more work than shadows.

### Shadows

Use rarely.

Avoid conventional floating-card shadows as a major brand device.

### Radius

Moderate and consistent.

Not:

-  completely hard-edged everywhere 
-  excessively rounded everywhere 

The system should combine crisp editorial geometry with selectively softer surfaces.

-  strong alignment

Use photography that feels:

-  architectural 
-  atmospheric 
-  food/hospitality aware 
-  contemporary 
-  commercially relevant 

Useful subjects:

-  restaurant interiors 
-  tables and dining spaces 
-  architectural details

Avoid relying solely on glossy luxury photography.

Photography should provide humanity and hospitality warmth against the more technical Mendglass system.

The brand itself must remain strong even when no photography is present.

# 17. Motion direction

Motion is not fully implemented yet, but the brand principle is established.

Mendglass motion should feel like:

**reveal · align · focus · slide · resolve**

Not:

-  bounce 
-  wobble 
-  excessive parallax 
-  playful cursor tricks 
-  constant movement 

A recurring idea may be:

**soft → sharp**

**misaligned → aligned**

**obscured → revealed**

Motion should reinforce clarity and refinement.

Exact timings and implementation belong in the Website & Portfolio build.

# 18. Section-theme principles

These are brand guardrails, **not fixed homepage assignments**.

Valid primary environments include:

### Mineral

Quiet default canvas.

### Warm Stone

Warm editorial/hospitality environment.

### Mist

Cooler technical contrast.

### Carbon

Primary high-contrast dark environment.

### Deep Mineral

Alternative dark environment with slightly more character.

Optical Accent should not normally function as an entire section theme.

The actual sequence of these themes on the Mendglass website will be decided in `04 — Website & Portfolio Build`.

# 19. Layout principles

Prefer:

-  generous whitespace 
-  deliberate asymmetry 
-  strong column/grid logic 
-  confident typography 
-  clear information hierarchy 
-  alternating editorial and system-like compositions 

Avoid:

-  generic agency grids repeated on every section 
-  excessive card layouts 
-  over-decoration 
-  visually dense interfaces without commercial reason 

---

# 20. Brand personality guardrails

Mendglass should feel:

**Competent**

Professional digital work is immediately credible.

**Clear**

Hierarchy and communication come before decoration.

**Crafted**

Details reward inspection.

**Technological**

Subtle digital/product sophistication without appearing to be an AI company.

**Commercially aware**

Design supports real customer journeys and business outcomes.

**Expandable**

Nothing about the core identity permanently restricts Mendglass to restaurant web design.

---

# 21. Avoid

Do not default to:

-  neon AI gradients 
-  purple/blue startup styling 
-  excessive glassmorphism 
-  glass shards as decoration 
-  generic abstract blobs 
-  meaningless 3D assets 
-  hyper-minimal “luxury” styling 
-  heavy SaaS card interfaces 
-  random monospace typography 
-  excessive rounded pills 
-  prominent shadows 
-  overuse of Optical Accent 
-  trend-driven redesign every time a new visual reference appears
